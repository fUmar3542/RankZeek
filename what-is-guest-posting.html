What is guest posting?

Guest posting means writing an article for another website in your field, in exchange for a link back to yours.

Guide

How it works

You pitch a useful topic to a site your audience reads. If it's accepted, you write the article and the site publishes it with an author bio or a link in the text.

Why it helps

A link from a trusted, relevant site tells search engines your page is worth showing. The article also puts your name in front of new readers.

What to avoid

Skip sites that publish anything for a fee, and don't stuff links into the text. One or two natural links in a strong article beat ten forced ones.
