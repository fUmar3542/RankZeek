How to choose guest post sites

Not every site is worth a link. These five checks help you pick the ones that are.

Strategy

Five checks

  • Topic fit: the site covers your niche.
  • Real traffic: visitors come from search, not only ads.
  • Clean history: no spam, casino or adult content.
  • Editorial standards: posts are edited and have real authors.
  • Indexed pages: new posts appear in Google within days.

Metrics are a guide

Domain authority is useful for comparing sites but it isn't a Google score. A smaller site with the right audience often beats a big, unrelated one.

Ask for proof

A good provider shows you the site list before you pay. At RankZeek, you approve every site first.
